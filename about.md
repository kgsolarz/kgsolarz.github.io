## About Me

I am a graduate student in the [Department of Statistical Science](https://stat.duke.edu/) at Duke University, where I am applying my passion for quantitative analysis to advance statistical methods in clinical cancer research. My research interests include Bayesian hierarchical modeling, Bayesian survival analysis, causal inference, and study design. 

I graduated magna cum laude from the University of Notre Dame with a B.S. in Applied and Computational Mathematics and Statistics and a second major in Economics. At Notre Dame, I was a member of the Varsity Cheerleading team, where I had the privilege of cheering on the Fighting Irish at varsity athletic events including football and basketball (pics below). Go Irish!

Outside of academics, I enjoy cooking, baking, lifting, & barre.
